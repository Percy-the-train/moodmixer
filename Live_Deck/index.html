PROTOTYPE DESIGN AND JUSTIFICATION

Three roads we did not take.

01 · VALVES FIRST · REJECTED
Tried one solenoid valve per flavour. Why not: seals leak, cleaning is slow, a failed valve drains the line. Chose peristaltic pumps: self-priming, tube-only wetted part.
02 · PI KIOSK · REJECTED
Tried a Raspberry Pi, screen and speaker as its face. Why not: OS image, drivers, lock-down, updates — a second machine. Chose computer + server: one page, no drivers.
03 · LASER-CUT SHELL · REJECTED
Why not: flat panels cannot form channels or pump cradles. Chose a printed shell with guides and a cup bay.
Thread
Every rejection moved complexity from hardware into testable software.

PROTOCOL · 115200 BAUD · HELLO · CAL <g> · DISPENSE <ch> <g> · STOPALL
HX711: DT = D12 · SCK = D13
Two logic pins: one sets direction, one switches the bridge. No PWM — dose is time plus weight; calibration lives in EEPROM.
DISPENSE CYCLE · ONE LOOP, SERIAL FIRST, SCALE SECOND
FAST→500 ms SETTLE→SLOW at 18 g→±2 g TOP-UP→DONE
Any fault (no flow · stall · overfill · sensor · link · timeout) → all outputs low → the kiosk shows why.
HARD LIMITS · TARGET +12 G HARD STOP · 60 PULSES · 3 S WATCHDOG · 300 S TIMEOUT · CALIBRATION IN EEPROM
Arduino Uno R3 — the platform this course teaches (L2, L5–6).
Why it fits: 5V logic, 14 digital pins, no OS.

The stack, close up.

01
Fluid drive
Run two zip ties per motor through the board; heads facing the outlet.
02
Wire floor
Label CH1–CH4 first; red on +. A swap pours the wrong ingredient.
03
Power stage
Four DRV8871 on standoffs. 12 V taps in parallel; two logic wires each; motor on OUT1/OUT2.
05
Control
Arduino on top. Tie its GND to the driver block — the one wire people leave out.
Check
With 12 V unplugged, tug every Dupont shell and every motor terminal. No bare copper, no reversed tap, no short between +12 V and GND.
